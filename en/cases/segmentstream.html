Marketing analytics SaaS platform, UK
my part

team
Web designer and illustrator
scope
10+ pages, responsive

task

The product had grown, and the visual style hadn't kept up. The company needed a website that is easy to extend with new features and solutions.

my role

  • Designed one page of the website and handed the project over to a web designer
  • Then led the web designer and the illustrator: reviewed their work and the quality of the layouts

what we did

  • Updated the key visual: deeper, more premium, more confident
  • Built a responsive design system of pages — features, solutions, pricing, case studies — ready for development
  • The illustrator created an illustration system that explains attribution and incrementality tests step by step

result

The website is built as a system: new pages are assembled from ready-made templates — by the team or by an AI agent.
